00 / Direction

PUREFORM’s direction.

Chosen to support a simple, friendly everyday shopping experience.

Audience hypotheses; not customer-tested

Audience

Young adults ages 18–25, especially college students who like minimalist fashion.

Looking for comfortable, simple, affordable shirts that are easy to match.

Archetype

Everyman

Friendly, relatable, simple, and approachable. PUREFORM feels familiar and made for everyday people.

Visual direction

Modernist

Clear hierarchy, simple forms, and organized sections make the T-shirt and useful information easy to find.
