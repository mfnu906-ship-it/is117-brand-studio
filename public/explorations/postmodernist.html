Direction B / Postmodernist study

A familiar object, remixed.

Reference
“Super lamp prototype,” designed by Martine Bedin for Memphis, made by Fausto Celati, 1981; V&A museum no. M.1-2011. I inspected the V&A image: a deep-blue wheeled body and a ring of brightly colored bulbs make a functional lamp read like a playful mobile object.
Design choices
  • Offset paper-like panels and overlapping frames create a deliberate, layered composition around the same shirt.
  • A serif display face beside clean sans-serif copy mixes visual languages while the headline and action stay legible.
  • Repeated circles and modular color blocks translate the lamp's wheel-and-bulb rhythm without copying the object.
Audience-effect hypothesis
The playful framing may help a wardrobe-builder imagine a simple basic as part of a personal, consistent style.
Risk
Too many layers could make the simple shirt feel complicated or more luxurious than PUREFORM intends.
