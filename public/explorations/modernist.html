Direction A / Modernist study

Function gives the page its rhythm.

Reference
“Drawing,” Edward McKnight Kauffer, 1934, England; V&A museum no. E.3770-2004. I inspected the V&A image: a figure assembled from repeating dark geometric forms sits on a pale ground along a strong diagonal axis.
Design choices
  • A measured two-column grid orders the headline, shirt, and action so the page scans from message to product to next step.
  • A spare diagonal rule and geometric marker translate the reference's directional composition without copying its figure.
  • High-contrast sans-serif type, clear scale, and open margins keep the information readable and easy to reproduce.
Audience-effect hypothesis
The organized hierarchy may help an everyday shopper understand the offer and act without sorting through decoration.
Risk
The strict grid could feel anonymous or austere for PUREFORM's friendly Everyman character.
The comfort and budget wording is learner-selected draft copy, not verified product information. The starter's $28 price is fictional demo data.
