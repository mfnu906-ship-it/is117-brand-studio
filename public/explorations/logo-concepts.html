Three Modular P Sketches

Rough, one-color symbol studies built from basic shapes. These are discussion concepts, not finished marks or approved artwork.

A · Corner-built

Four joined bars form a compact P with a small open center. Strong silhouette; may feel blocky.

B · Open counter

A clear vertical stem and two separated bowl bars imply a P through negative space. Airy; may read as unfinished.

C · Tile-built

Equal square tiles assemble into a pixel-like P. Memorable rhythm; may feel digital or overly rigid.
