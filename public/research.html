Research behind
the everyday tee.

A first comparison of clothing websites, possible brand positions, and audience hypotheses. These audience ideas are assumptions, not validated customer research.

01 / Competitor research

Three clothing websites.

Each observation is an initial interpretation of the public website, not a claim about every customer.

UNIQLO ↗

Reviewed November 5, 2026

Observation: UNIQLO presents everyday clothing with an emphasis on practical basics, simple styling, and wearable colors. Product pages help shoppers compare styles and product details.

What it suggests for PUREFORM: Keep the T-shirt design simple and make product information easy to scan. Show how one basic shirt can work with different everyday outfits.

Audience, character, and look: The presentation suggests a practical, straightforward position and a clean, product-focused look. This is my interpretation of the site.

H&M ↗

Reviewed November 5, 2026

Observation: H&M presents clothing across different styles and trends, including casual basics. Product images, categories, and outfit styling help shoppers browse options.

What it suggests for PUREFORM: Stay affordable and simple while showing clear choices such as color and fit. The site can feel current without too many competing visual elements.

Audience, character, and look: The broad assortment and trend-led presentation suggest accessible fashion and a more varied visual style than PUREFORM's planned minimal direction. This is an interpretation.

Cotton On ↗

Reviewed November 5, 2026

Observation: Cotton On presents casual clothing and everyday looks with a relaxed, youthful visual style. Product and outfit imagery helps communicate how items might be worn.

What it suggests for PUREFORM: Use friendly, approachable language and everyday styling examples. Product images should help customers imagine wearing the T-shirt in normal situations.

Audience, character, and look: The presentation suggests a casual lifestyle position and approachable tone. PUREFORM can borrow the relaxed feeling while keeping its own website cleaner and more minimal. This is an interpretation.

02 / Positioning

Two possible positions.

PUREFORM needs a clear reason to exist in the everyday T-shirt category.

Affordable everyday basics

Comfortable, simple T-shirts at a reasonable price for regular use. This may suit students and young adults who want easy-to-match clothing without spending too much.

Premium minimalist essentials

Minimalist T-shirts presented as a more exclusive, higher-priced wardrobe choice. This may suit shoppers who prioritize a premium image and a consistent minimalist wardrobe.

Chosen: affordable everyday basics

This fits PUREFORM's current promise of comfortable, affordable, simple everyday T-shirts. The premium position is less suitable because the brand should feel accessible rather than expensive or exclusive.

03 / Audience hypotheses

Two possible people.

These are planning hypotheses, not validated customer research.

Persona A — College student

A college student aged 18–25 who attends classes, meets friends, and wants a comfortable, affordable T-shirt that is easy to match with everyday outfits.

Possible concern: Will a basic shirt work for different casual situations while staying within budget?

Persona B — Minimalist wardrobe builder

A young adult aged 18–25 who prefers a consistent, simple wardrobe and wants versatile basics that coordinate with clothes they already own.

Possible concern: Will the shirt work with outfits they already have?

Chosen audience

Young adults ages 18–25, especially college students. This audience matches the affordable everyday position and the situations described in the PUREFORM brand brief. The choice still needs peer feedback or later customer research.

04 / Brand and persuasion

Simple, friendly, honest.

Research should connect to visible decisions in the Brand Studio.

Brand character

PUREFORM uses the Everyman archetype and a friendly, simple, approachable voice. It should feel relatable and useful for everyday people, not luxurious or intimidating.

Visual direction

Modernist: clean layout, clear hierarchy, simple typography, open spacing, neutral colors, and rust-red accents. This is intended to make the product and shopping information easy to understand.

Honest persuasion

Use accurate descriptions, clear prices, realistic images, and visible size choices. Do not invent reviews, limited stock, discounts, material details, sustainability claims, or product-performance claims. Styling suggestions should be optional and should not block shopping.

05 / Assumptions

What still needs checking.

Keep assumptions visible so they are not mistaken for research findings.

  • I assume college students value affordability and easy-to-match basics; I have not validated this with a survey.
  • I assume a clean, minimal website will make the product easier to understand; this needs peer or user feedback.
  • I have not confirmed whether the audience prefers regular or oversized fit.
  • I have not established what price customers would actually pay.

Peer feedback and the resulting decision are documented in the project research file and GitHub issues. The audience remains a hypothesis and needs further customer research.
